conference schedule

enfold conference will be held September 21-22, 2023 at the campus of the Burroughs Wellcome Fund (North Carolina, USA).

day 1

09.21.2023

  • Convening of attendees: ice breaker 2 sentences on what first inspired your passion for your given field?

    Dan Jay Introduction and Charge (15 min)

    Values: humility, openness, and mutual respect

    Talks: Five speakers (Arthur Miller, Ahna Skop, Victoria Vesna, Michael John Gorman, Mauro Martino, 15 min each). They will address: the value of the Art-Science culture (Miller); How does Art benefit Science (Skop); how does Science benefit Art (Vesna); Art-Science institutes in Europe and their successes and challenges (Gorman); and Art-Science in Industry (Martino).

  • break into three groups with artists and scientists intermingled to address an unsolved problem bringing openness and teachability with no experts.

    1. Climate Change

    2. Understanding Complexity

    3. Global Health and Economic Complexity

    4. Consciousness and Artificial Intelligence

  • informal session on How I think (art vs. science led by Suzanne Barbour)
