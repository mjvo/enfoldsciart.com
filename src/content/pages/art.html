Illuminating Intersections

Each participant will decorate a slotted triangle paper piece to represent the intersection of science and art. Each triangle will represent each participant’s reflection on the following prompt:

What is needed to grow and sustain community at the intersection of art and science?

Together, the triangles will slot together to form collaborative 8-triangle polygon lanterns. These lanterns will be on display for the conference.
